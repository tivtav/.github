# Hi there, we're tivtav 👋

**The help dental practices were missing.** 🦷

tivtav is the operational hub for dental practices. Documents, inventory, equipment, team and locations all live in one place, so practice owners spend less time on admin and more time on their patients.

## ✨ What we build

- 📄 **Documents & protocols**: document management, protocols and compliance
- 📦 **Inventory & suppliers**: always know what's in stock and what's on order
- 🛠️ **Equipment**: maintenance, follow-up and history for every device
- 👩‍⚕️ **Team & time**: team management, time tracking and capacity planning
- 🏥 **Locations & rooms**: one overview for every practice and treatment room
- 🗓️ **Planning & communication**: practice calendar, tasks and a shared team workspace
- 🤖 **Assistant**: software that thinks along with you
- 📊 **Insights**: analytics and reporting to keep your practice on track

## 💙 What we care about

- **Clarity.** Software should make your day calmer.
- **Less friction.** Fewer clicks, fewer spreadsheets, fewer sticky notes.
- **Real support.** Personal onboarding and monthly check-ins, not just a help center.
